Privacy policy

Paper Studio PDF Scanner
Updated 15 October 2026

About this policy

Clearleaf Apps provides Paper Studio PDF Scanner, installed as Paper Studio: Document Scanner & PDF Maker. This policy explains how the Android app handles your information.

Your documents stay under your control

The app processes selected photos and PDF pages on your device to crop, straighten, enhance, preview, and create PDFs. It does not send your documents to Clearleaf Apps or a scanning server. No account is required. This version has no advertising, analytics, tracking, or automatic crash-reporting service operated by Clearleaf Apps.

Camera, gallery, and file selection

The app uses Android camera and file/photo selection interfaces when you choose those actions. It reads the photos and PDFs you select. System interfaces and your camera or document provider may apply their own permissions and privacy practices. Imported originals are not overwritten.

Local storage and retention

Saved documents are kept in the app library with information such as their filename, creation time, page count, and reference to an exported copy. Working images are held for editing, and temporary copies may remain in app or system caches until cleared. Saved library PDFs remain until you delete them or remove app data. Photos to PDF preserves the supplied image bytes; do not assume that image metadata has been removed.

Saving, sharing, and other services

You choose where to export a PDF and whether to share it through another app. A location selected in Android may belong to a cloud provider. Selected recipients, camera apps, document providers, and sharing apps handle data under their own policies. Clearleaf Apps does not receive a copy through these actions. Android device backup or device transfer may also include app data depending on your device and settings; the app does not operate its own cloud backup service.

Deleting documents

The library deletion flow can remove the app copy and its linked exported copy when Android grants access and the provider confirms deletion. If access is unavailable, the app explains how to locate the copy or remove only the library copy. Removing app data or uninstalling does not remove separately exported files. Original photos, original PDFs, copies shared elsewhere, provider trash, and independent backups must be managed separately.

Security

The library uses Android app storage and access controls. Exported and shared files depend on the protections of the location or recipient you choose. The app does not add password protection or encryption to the PDFs it creates.

Public policy website

The public copy of this policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting that website may allow GitHub to process connection information such as your IP address and browser information under its own privacy statement at https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement. Clearleaf Apps has not added analytics or tracking scripts to that page. You can also read this policy inside the app without opening a website.

Support and policy updates

For support or privacy questions, contact clearleafapps@gmail.com. If you email us, we receive the address, message, and any attachments you send, and use them to respond and resolve the request. Our email provider handles those messages under its own policies. We retain support correspondence as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations; you may ask us to delete it. Changes to app data handling will be reflected in this policy and the store disclosures before they apply.
